## Capability-tier evidence

- **Fully supported (shipped, no warning):** heading hierarchy + style set; page setup;
  headers/footers + page numbers incl. first-page/odd-even; TOC (no page numbers); inline
  images; regular/merged-cell/nested tables; captions; content cross-references; footnotes;
  basic equations.
- **Supported with warning / downgrade:** floating images → downgraded to centred inline
  (reported); page-number restart → applied and warned; `PAGEREF` → downgraded to a content
  reference (reported); multi-column layout → warned if present in an output.
- **Out of scope (refused, not attempted):** content editing (ADR-0002 — content text is
  byte/word-identical after every run); in-place editing (ADR-0003 — output is always new);
  unknown/unimplemented constructs (e.g. charts) rejected with a usage error.

## Known limitations / unverified

- **Formatting-requirement overrides without a template** are recorded as `unverified` (no
  style source to apply them against); the WordFlow defaults are used. This is reported, not
  faked.
- **Automated restructuring** is not part of v0.1; a confirmed restructure request is a
  reported downgrade and the structure/content are left unchanged (spec D5.5).
- **Floating images** are never shipped floating (downgraded to inline); this is a reported
  downgrade, matching the compatibility finding that LibreOffice renders anchored images in
  flow.
- **Pagination is not pixel-identical across applications** (e.g. the full-feature document
  is 2 pages in Word, 1 in WPS); the promise is *opens without repair* (ADR-0004), not
  identical rendering.
- **Font substitution** on a host lacking SimSun/SimHei is the OS's behaviour and reported
  where relevant (#8).
